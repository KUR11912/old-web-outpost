## 复现

```powershell
$qaNode = (Get-Command node).Source
$qaPlaywright = Join-Path (Split-Path (Split-Path $qaNode -Parent) -Parent) 'node_modules\playwright\index.mjs'
& $qaNode qa/sky-browser-test.mjs $qaPlaywright http://127.0.0.1:5180/
& $qaNode qa/sky-photo-test.mjs $qaPlaywright http://127.0.0.1:5180/
```

grass-01.wav through grass-05.wav derive from footstep_grass_000.ogg through
footstep_grass_004.ogg; stone-01.wav through stone-05.wav derive from
footstep_concrete_000.ogg through footstep_concrete_004.ogg.

Edits: converted to mono 44.1 kHz / 16-bit PCM WAV; removed DC offset and excess
silence; applied 2 ms attack / 12 ms release fades; normalized peaks to -6 dBFS.
The concrete hard-surface steps are used for the game's stone paths and bridge.
